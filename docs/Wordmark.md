# Wordmark

The Anser brand mark: lowercase `anser.` — the full stop is the fixed brand device (the answer, given) and is **always amber**. Never recolour or remove the stop, never capitalise the name.

```tsx
<Wordmark size={32} />
<Wordmark size={28} sub="EVERY CALL, ANSWERED" />
<Wordmark variant="icon" size={40} />
<Wordmark variant="lockup" size={30} />
```

`icon` is the app icon (navy speech bubble, amber dot) for favicons and avatars; `lockup` pairs it with the wordmark for headers.
